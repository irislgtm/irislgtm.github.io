# Some of my opinions


<!-- date: 2026-10-21 -->

This has been my third or fourth entry to this blog, and this one is going to be about some of my opinions on the Minecraft modding community that I have acquired over my many years of being a part of it. This blog may appear a bit different from my other blogs as I am dictating it from voice to text, so it might be a little different, but I hope that it is still legible nonetheless.

So, when I started modding, I used r/feedthebeast servers and other popular feed the beast related reddits and discords to join different servers. I had initially only played Create and some other 1.12.2 packs with some people I found on the feed the beast discord, but later I slowly joined more and more servers as I wanted to find out about more and more mods. Initially, I joined the GregTech CEU server, which had a large history behind it, but most of the developers were aged and definitely not of my age group, so it was hard to relate with them. Plus, I was kind of annoying back then and had some horrible sense in humor, such that nobody in that server liked me or trusted me and I eventually left there after a while.

Besides that, I have also been in the NuclearCraft server, which was also a good experience compared to the other servers I have been in. They have a very welcoming community and were generally much nicer than the GregTech CEU ones, although I hope this won't cause any drama in the future if one of them reads this. But anyways, I have also been in the NTM discord community. I imagine they don't like me very much over there, but..  it is quite fun, and I have met some good people on there who have similar ways of thinking. There has also been the Just Enough Opinions discord server, and I have some mixed feelings on it. First of all, when I was a modpack developer, I did not realize what kind of experiences that mod developers go through, I find them to be kind of pretentious, but not in a very harmful way. It feels kind of annoying to interact with them because they have their own community that is kind of walled off to others unless you match their way of thinking exactly. They also seem to only care about things that relate to them, which is understandable, but most of them don't actually relate or appreciate things that don't come into their realm of comfort. For example, I made a mod pack, I mean a scripting mod, for 1.7.10, and the leader of that community was acting kind of aloof towards it even though it wasn't relating to him. But why do you have to specifically say that you don't care and that 1.7.10 is a trash version or whatever. Like, let people do what they want. So, that kind of left a negative impact on me from then. Otherwise, I don't mind them too much. I was pretty annoying back then myself, so it is understandable as to why they wouldn't like me. Other than that, I have had my own community for a while. There was originally Quantum Studios, which I had started in 2024, I think, but that was for the GT Quantum mod pack. GT Quantum is in fact what started my entire modding career, as you would say. So, I had a small community of developers back then. Although I don't meet them very much nowadays, it wasn't that bad. I'd say it was pretty friendly to me and to the mod pack itself, although the mod pack wasn't that good, but that is kind of besides the point. So, ever since I left and rejoined Quantum Studios, I renamed it to Trifectum. Trifectum means basically, well, I mean it refers to the three arrows that show up on my profile picture. You can see my other article for the meaning of that. But these three arrows are really meaningful to me. Basically, I put them wherever I go. And originally, the logo for Trifectum was supposed to be three arrows like this in the original color scheme, which was the Windows 95 blue and gray color scheme. I had chosen that because my first actual game was called Ergographia, and I had made that based on a Windows 95 theme, and it introduced me to the entire visual style of Windows 95 that I still quite enjoy to this day. It is low effort and helps get high-quality looking results without having to put in many assets.  

Other than that, I've been a part of the legacy modding community for 1.7.10 for around a few months, I think since June 2026. I've been there before but I left and rejoined because of some purposes but regardless, I find that community to be kind of interesting and both kind of annoying as well because most people there act quite pretentious. But other than that, there are only a few other communities that I've been in, and my opinions of them are generally not positive. First, I'd say there is the 1.12.2 coalition which acts as kind of a worse version of legacy modding, I would say. No offense to the 1.12 coalition people but they are kind of disingenuous, I find that. It's kind of a hard feeling to place but I just don't like them, I think. But other than that, there is also the Cleanroom Discord which I despise. Originally, I joined it for learning 1.12.2 mods and pack development But I slowly found out over time how much that place actually sucked. Even though people who praise that community argue that whatever the leader says about children is just fiction and fiction should be differentiated from reality, I still find that it is an incredibly toxic place for basically no reason. Even though they have never made content mods to the ones I have known of, and usually focus on performance mods, they have an incredibly pretentious attitude towards content mods and certain developers. Other than that, they are very racist, and I mean a high degree of racist. Like, I am Indian as you would know, and they called me their pet and constantly spammed me with like Pajeet memes and other racist content.

But other than that, they also have some extremely weird conversations with each other about children or lolis in general, and I think that sexualizing children like that is generally kind of questionable. And even though you could say that they are fictional, sexualizing them in any context is horrible, I would say.

Other than that, I have not been part of many other communities in modding. There have been a few servers which I have been a part of, but those are usually inconsequential and usually only for like playing on the game rather than the developing for it.

I usually stay quite reserved from these servers, and whenever I try to show my content over there, I try to show it in a way that does not like entrench me into the community too much. I usually keep to myself and to the NTM Discord as well as my personal Discord, and yeah, I do not step out of it that much.
