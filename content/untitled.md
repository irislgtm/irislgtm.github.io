# Cancel culture and drama with leclowndu in the MMC community

Hello. This is my sixth entry to this blog and in this entry I am going to be talking about some experiences I have had with cancel culture and how it is affecting modded Minecraft in this state.

Cancel culture is a thing that has been going on in the internet for quite a while now. Originally, when we think of cancel culture, we think of things like if a rapper or if a musician has said something controversial on social media, they would be apprehended for that and their content would be socially boycotted in a way.

This can apply to public figures, in my opinion, but for more private people or small communities, this can also be quite harmful, especially when the person has said something many years ago that they regret now and have apologized for, which in this case can be seen in the Thamaturge Minecraft drama.

So, before we begin with the drama itself, I would like to talk about the developer of the mod. Thamaturge was developed by a person called LeclownDo91350, who has been in the GregTech modding community for the past few years or who had been there at least because he had gotten banned in 2023. Why had he gotten banned? Well, it was because he had said some extremely disturbing things to one of the members of the GregTech CEO developer community, which caused him to get banned. Considering his other inappropriate behavior there, it was warranted at that time, but in my opinion, it is an important thing to consider that this was in 2023. Taking his current age into account, which is 19 or 18, from what I have seen on his website, 3 years ago he would have been 16, and that is an age where people make a lot of stupid decisions and say a lot of stupid things in general.

So I think that rather than reprimanding him for this mistake for the entirety of his career, we should instead focus on the fact that he has apologized. I do know that you have to apologize to someone and have them accept your apology, and it may be possible that he is being disingenuous, but I believe that he has done all he can without making the situation worse for himself, and that he may actually regret what he has said in the past, and it is probably time to let that go. It is quite unfortunate to see that someone like him still is reprimanded for his actions 3 years after the fact.

In my opinion, no matter the words he said, he did not commit an actual crime. I mean, it could be classified as hate speech in a way, but again, he was a minor at that time, and we all have said some stupid stuff in the past ourselves. So, that is basically the drama surrounding him. I'd say it is overblown to this date. I was participating in it too, and I have done quite a bit of research. Most of the people who I have asked have generally said that he has improved over the past few years, and he has both apologized and reformed. So, I don't think it is prudent to drag this drama along onto him much further. Plus, I also doubt that the Grectic CEO developers have a very clean past themselves, from what I've heard, although that conversation is for another time. Other than that, I'd like to talk about the main copyright-related drama that is been going on. So, the gist of it is that once Thomcraft 6 was done in 2018, the main developer of Thomcraft left the development permanently. He hadn't responded to anyone in a while, but he had given permissions to COFH, It is a popular modding group that has been existing for quite a while now. They have made a lot of old mods like COFH Core and  also made mods like Thermal Expansion, which pioneered tech mods back in 1.12.2 and 1.7.10.
However, he only gave rights to them to port Thaumcraft 6 to modern Minecraft in the form of Thaumcraft 7. He did not give them any access to the original full rights over Thaumcraft, and Azanor, who is the original developer of the mod, still retains full licenses over the mod. It was just a simple permission that he had given to them to allow them to port the mod. So they had been working on this for a few years now, but due to their own difficulties and managing their other mods, they have been unable to develop it for quite a while. Considering that he had left in 2018, it has been almost 8 years, I'd say, since Azanor has disappeared, and it has been quite a long time since we have seen any progress on Thaumcraft 7.

From C-U-F-H, we have seen a lot of screenshots and teaser trailers with custom art and possibly new mechanics being made for the new Thaumcraft version. But there was also a document released by C-U-F-H which detailed exactly how their dev process works. So, they had detailed in this doc about how they were unable to develop Thaumcraft 7 for quite a while and why they were not hiring any more people into their team. Apparently, there have been multiple incidents in which people who have volunteered for making Thaumcraft 7 hadn't contributed at all into the mod which disheartened the C-U-F-H team. Other than that, there was also an incident a couple of years ago where the developer for the mod Malum was alleged to have stolen Thaumcraft 7 concepts and code and have used it in his own mod while feigning that he would help in Thaumcraft 7 development. This was a couple of years ago though, and C-U-F-H had also admitted back then that they had preemptively done a copyright strike on Malum without having conclusive evidence of his wrongdoing. This kind of shows a pattern with C-U-F-H being kind of sensitive towards these things. So, I would say that this pattern definitely applies to the modern Thaumcraft 7 issues which we will get to now. So, the guy which I have been talking about called Leglown, had recently made a Thaumcraft port to modern Minecraft called Thaumaturge. This mod was less of a regional remake of Thaumcraft as Thaumcraft 7 was intended to be, but rather a direct port of the mod to modern Minecraft from 1.7.10. It ported Thaumcraft 4 which was considered the better version of the mod. And there have been, there was probably A-I used in the porting of the mod, which I wouldn't say is much of a big deal because most of the large modders use A-I nowadays, and it only really matters when the content is visibly low effort. This topic is a wildfire in the modding community right now though, and it has many different angles and people arguing for many different sides. So, I would say it is not that easy to judge a mod just based on what if it used A-I or not. There is also almost effort was put into it by the developer himself, how they developed, what used assets, and how much experience the developer has.

However, I would say that this port is quite a high-quality port compared to low-effort AI ports of this mod, and it is generally one of the better ones as it also planned to adapt the mod properly into modern instead of doing sloppy porting with AI directly doing everything for the mod. It is one of the better uses of AI in modding, and the developer himself has also been a modder for a decent amount of time before AI has existed. About AI, I have used AI in the past for mod developing. I would say that it had been a bad habit for me in the past, around 2026, early 2026, but I had grown out of it slowly over time, and now I don't use it as much.

In early 2025, I had been developing mods on my own using Java directly, and I had begun using AI and started relying on it heavily during the time I had made my GregTech fork, which is probably visible in the fork's quality as well. I was struggling with the fees of the AI, and was also struggling with ethical questions about whether it was right to use it or not. The one-month break I took after developing that GregTech fork was essential for disconnecting with AI and helping myself learn those old skills again, and now I am back to being a decently advanced developer of modded Minecraft without using AI all that much. Although I will say that I do use local models every now and again to do repetitive tasks or do security checks that I don't want to do myself. It is quite useful in that aspect, but I would say that it is not good to rely on it completely, and it shows in the effort and the quality of the mod itself if you use it wrongly. But anyways, what I mean to say is that Thaumaturge is not a low-effort slop port, and it certainly is one of the better ports I've seen across these years.

After that, we have to get to the main reason COFH is copyrighted Thaumaturge, and that was because it was mostly copied over from Thaumcraft 4. The thing is that Thaumaturge had a lot of assets and code that were directly ported from Thaumcraft 6 and 4. I think it was mostly 4, but anyways, Thaumcraft 4 had a lot of matching assets, although most of these were open source or MIT assets that matched with Thaumcraft Thaumaturge, and there has been a lot of code that was reused between the versions. It is not is a proper remake, in the sense that Thaumcraft 7 was but it was decently good for being a port using AI. However, the recent noise about this port has caused COFH to take advantage of this and convince the big modding studios, that is ATM and FTB, to not use the mod at all in their modpacks. And as they control a lot of modding Minecraft, it is quite concerning that they have such a monopoly over these mods. What I am about to say next is from a conversation I had with the developer himself. And that was regarding some statements he made that said that the ATM and FTB team had told him to give up development on the mod, because he as a person was too controversial and unstable to continue developing the mod in their eyes, and that if they did not follow his directions, they would kill the mod, or not use it in their packs, not letting it get off in general. Now, I think that is a pretty scummy move if that is actually true. If it is not true, then I would imagine that the mod itself would be quite successful on its own, like a couple I have seen in the past that are not included in the big packs. The big packs do not include Meleum for the large part. And if the mod itself is enough has enough quality, then why wouldn't they? They have also been entrenched in transphobic issues themselves regarding to ATM, so why would they be concerned about such a thing? It is quite odd to say the least. Other than that, COFH is acting quite scummy about it in general, because they don't have the right to do any copyright strikes on them. They only received permission to port Thaumcraft 7, and they don't have the original rights over them. It is within a reasonable bound to assume that they have to port it to the platforms, which is within their realm of ability, but a direct copyright strike from them is not feasible in my eyes. I am sure that if they are reading this, they had something to say. I would like for them to release the exact list of infractions that have been made, but otherwise from my time interviewing the developer on the Thaumcraft mod, I doubt that that is the case. In general, I hope that this mod gets released, as I feel bad for the developer and how he has been treated. I hope that FTB and ATM try to be more rational about this, and in general I hope for this mod to be successful. Thank you for reading, and goodbye.
